Outside model comparisons

Use these independent resources to investigate disagreements. They do not change CB Points.

CFB model performance

Andrew Percival’s CFB Picker ↗ compares projection systems using straight-up accuracy, ATS accuracy and prediction error, with closing-line context. Its standings rank models, not teams. Raw-data exports are disabled by the author.

CFB team consensus

CFB Metrics Consensus ↗ is the author’s separate ensemble for evaluating FBS teams. Treat consensus and constituent models as correlated benchmarks, not independent votes.

How we evaluate comparisons

Compare identical games and pregame timestamps. Track sample size, margin MAE and RMSE alongside ATS and straight-up records. Closing-line value requires an archived pregame line and a verified closing line. A rank-only list cannot be converted directly into point spreads.

What can influence production?

Collection is broad; promotion is narrow. Rankings are useful diagnostics, not a shortcut around validation.
