How to read this

A +7.0 rating means seven points above an average team on a neutral field. A rating difference is a team-strength comparison; venue and matchup context belong in the game projection.

Market rank and rating gap stay blank when the quote history cannot connect the whole league. Upcoming matchup gaps compare dated game estimates with observed lines. They are not national market-rating gaps or betting recommendations.

Rank uncertainty is not calibrated. Small differences should not be treated as decisive. Weekly movement will appear only after comparable snapshots exist.
